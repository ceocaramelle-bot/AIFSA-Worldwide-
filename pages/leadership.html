OUR LEADERSHIP

Leadership is service.

AIFSA is guided by women who are committed to service, responsibility, unity and the advancement of female students. Our leadership structure brings together experienced matrons and student leaders who work together to advance the association.

This page serves as the official digital directory of AIFSA leadership and will be updated as new leadership administrations take office.

Integrity Leading with honesty and accountability.
Service Putting the interests of members first.
Excellence Pursuing meaningful results and impact.
Sisterhood Building unity among our members.
