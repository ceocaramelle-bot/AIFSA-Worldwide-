OUR NETWORK

One association. Many communities.

AIFSA connects Akwa Ibom female students across institutions and locations, creating a network of sisterhood, leadership, opportunity and shared purpose.

THE AIFSA NETWORK

Your institution. Your chapter.

AIFSA chapters provide a local community through which members can connect with one another, participate in activities and access opportunities while remaining part of the wider AIFSA family.

Each chapter serves as a bridge between the association and female students within its institution or community. Chapters can organize initiatives, encourage participation, support members and promote the values of AIFSA.

✦

Connected by purpose.

Wherever an AIFSA member studies or serves, she remains part of a larger network of women committed to growth, excellence and sisterhood.
